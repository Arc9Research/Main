Open experiments and questions

Arc-9 Research shares work-in-progress ideas about how bodies and minds relate—especially connective tissue, hypermobility, and cognition. This page lists experiments we would like to run, data we wish existed, and places where outside help would move things forward. Nothing here is settled science; these are honest invitations to test ideas together.

About

Arc-9 Research is led by Grayson Kernan, an independent researcher in Washington State without a formal degree, learning in public. The work sits at the overlap of embodied cognition, connective tissue biology, and methods that let small teams ask big questions. The tone here is slow to claim and quick to revise when data disagree.

If you have already run a related study, hold a dataset, or want to fund or collaborate on one of the threads below, we would like to hear from you—see Get involved.
