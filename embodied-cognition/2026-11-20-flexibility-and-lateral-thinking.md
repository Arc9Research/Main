# Body flexibility and flexible thinking

**Posted:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Designing  
**Tags:** embodied cognition · hypermobility · psychology · behavioral

## Open question

Does body flexibility (hypermobility, measured with a self-administered Beighton score and the 5-Part Questionnaire) relate to flexible or lateral thinking (Alternate Uses Task, Remote Associates Test, task switching)?

## Why it matters

This is part of an embodied-cognition sketch: the brain may use the physical relationships of body tissues as analogies when building conceptual relationships. If joint and tissue flexibility correlate with cognitive flexibility, that would be one testable bridge—not proof of the model, but a place to look.
