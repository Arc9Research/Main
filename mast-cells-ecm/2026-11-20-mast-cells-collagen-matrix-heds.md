# Mast cells and collagen matrix in hEDS

**Posted:** 2026-11-20  
**Status:** Seeking data  
**Tags:** hEDS · mast cells · collagen · immunology

## Open question

Can mast cell activity (tryptase, chymase, hereditary alpha-tryptasemia) disrupt collagen matrix assembly in hypermobile Ehlers–Danlos syndrome (hEDS)?

## Why it matters

Mast cell involvement is often discussed alongside hypermobility, but mechanistic links to collagen organization are still thin. If mast-cell mediators alter matrix assembly, that could connect immune signals to tissue fragility without requiring a single collagen gene answer for every patient.
